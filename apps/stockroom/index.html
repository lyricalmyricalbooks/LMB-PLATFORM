Getting started

What is this app for?

It keeps track of your book: how many copies are left, every sale, every free copy you give away, the shops holding your books, and money you've spent. You and the publisher see the same numbers, so nobody has to keep a separate spreadsheet.

Where do I start?
  1. Open Dashboard to see how your book is doing.
  2. Sold a copy yourself? Go to Manual entry and add it.
  3. At a fair? Open 📲 My QR Code and let buyers scan it.

That's all most artists ever need.

What do the tabs along the top do?
  • Dashboard — your book at a glance: copies left, sales and earnings.
  • Manual entry — add a sale or a free copy you gave away.
  • Consignment — shops holding your books and what they owe.
  • History — every sale ever recorded.
  • Expenses — costs you paid for the book (to be paid back).
  • 📲 My QR Code — a payment code for buyers at events.
  • ❓ Help — this page.
Do I need to save?

No. Everything saves by itself the moment you add it. You'll see a small ✓ Saved note.

Selling books

How do I record a sale I made?
  1. Tap Manual entry.
  2. Type how many copies (Qty) and the price of one copy.
  3. Pick where you sold it under Channel (In person, Book fair…).
  4. Under Payment type, pick who got the money.
  5. Press the add button. Done!

Which “Payment type” do I pick?
  • The buyer paid you (cash, your e-transfer…) → directly to artist. You'll send the publisher their part later.
  • The buyer paid the publisher (QR code, website) → directly to publisher.
Why does my sale say it's waiting for approval?

When money went to you directly, the publisher gets a note to check it. That's normal — it doesn't mean anything is wrong. It will show as approved once they look at it.

I gave a copy away for free. What do I do?

Go to Manual entry and use the free copy (gratuity) form. Write who got it, how many, and the date. This keeps the copy count right.

I made a mistake in a sale.

Don't add a second sale to “cancel” it. Send the publisher a quick message with the date and amount and they'll fix it, so the totals stay correct.

Book fairs & events

How do buyers pay me at a fair?
  1. Open 📲 My QR Code.
  2. Show the code on your phone (or print it).
  3. The buyer scans it with their phone camera and pays by card.

The money goes straight to the publisher and the sale appears on its own — you don't need to type anything.

Can I print my QR code?

Yes. Press Download QR and print the picture, or save it to your phone's photos. Copy link gives you the web link to share online.

Someone paid me in cash at the fair.

Add it in Manual entry with Channel: Book fair and Payment type: directly to artist.

Shops & money

What is “Consignment”?

When a shop takes your books and pays only for the ones it sells. The Consignment tab shows each shop, how many copies it still has, and what it owes.

I paid for something for my book. Can I get it back?

Yes. Add it in Expenses with the amount, date and a short note (a photo of the receipt helps). The publisher reviews it and pays you back.

Where do I see how much I've earned?

On the Dashboard. For each single sale, look in History.

My Dashboard says I have money to send to the publisher.

When a buyer paid you directly, part of that money is the publisher's share. The Money to send to your publisher card shows how much.

If the publisher also owes you earnings, the two are already subtracted from each other, so there's only one payment to make. Press the Pay button on the card to pay exactly that amount by card — it's recorded for you, no message needed.

If the card says Nothing to send, there's nothing for you to pay right now.

Running the press

What can I see that artists can’t?

Artists only see their own book and the everyday tabs: Dashboard, Manual entry, Consignment, History, Expenses and their QR code. You see every book, plus the business tabs — Website orders, Event POS, Payments, Tax Centre, Customers, Shipping, Big Cartel, Sheets and Backups.

How do I see exactly what an artist sees?

Choose their book, then press Author view at the top of the screen. A note reminds you that you’re previewing. Press it again to get your full view back.

Where do I start each day?
  1. Open Dashboard and read any Action required note first.
  2. Open Website orders and apply the ones marked ready.
  3. Check To-do for anything still waiting on you.

How do I get to the website’s admin?

Use the Website · Inventory switch: at the top of the left menu on a computer, the Website ↗ button at the top of the screen on a phone or tablet, or the top of More. The red number on it is how many website orders are paid and not sent yet.

On a computer the website opens in its own tab, and pressing Inventory there brings you back to this tab — no pile of new tabs. A website sale in History has an Open on the website link that goes straight to that order, and the website’s orders link back here the same way.

It needs the internet: offline, the switch says so and opens nothing.

How do I switch between books?

Use the book picker at the top. Some tabs need one book chosen; others, like the Dashboard, can show all books together.

Sales & orders

An artist’s sale says it’s waiting for approval.

When a buyer paid the artist directly, the sale waits for you so the money owed between you stays correct. The Dashboard shows an Action required note with how many are waiting. Check the amount with the artist, then approve it.

How do website orders get into the books?

Open Website orders. Orders that match a book are marked ready — apply them and the copies and sales update on their own. Anything that can’t be matched stays listed so nothing is lost.

Why does my To-do list show website jobs?

Your shop’s website and this app share one to-do list. Under Waiting on your website you see what waits for you in the website’s admin — stock waiting for your OK, new books to match, website orders not sent yet — each with an Open on the website ↗ button. It clears by itself once it’s done there. The website shows what waits here in the same way.

Adding a book that’s already on the website

Books your website sells that this app doesn’t have are listed on the Website orders card under On the website, not in this app yet. Press Add to the app: the Add new book form opens with the title, author, ISBN and price filled in. Add the print run, check the rest and save. The website then links its book to the new one by itself. Nothing is added until you save, and Not a book for the app stops it being offered again.

A book’s title, author, ISBN or price is different on the website

The Website orders card lists them under Details that differ from the website, for every book linked to the website. Each line names the book and the detail, and shows Website: and This app: side by side. Use the website’s copies the website’s wording or price into this app’s book (only this app changes — never the website). Leave as is keeps yours; that line comes back only if one side changes again. Prices are only compared for books priced in Canadian dollars, and ISBN and price only when one website edition is linked to the book.

Finding a book or a website order quickly

Press the 🔍 button at the top of the screen (or Ctrl K / ⌘ K, or / on a keyboard) and start typing a title, an author, an ISBN or a website order number. It finds the books in this app, the books on your website and the website orders this app holds. Books in this app open here; website books and orders open on the website, in its own tab. The last line, Search the website for “…”, runs the same words through the website admin’s search.

An artist made a mistake in a sale.

Find the original sale in History and correct that one, rather than adding a second sale to cancel it out. That keeps the copy count and what each person is owed correct.

How do I sell at a fair or event?

Open Event POS on your phone or tablet. It’s built for quick sales at a table, keeps the screen awake, and keeps working without internet — sales send once you’re back online.

How much should I charge for shipping?

Open Shipping. Save the size and weight of the packages you usually mail, then compare live postage prices (Canada Post options are highlighted). It also suggests what to charge customers for website shipping, so postage doesn’t eat into each sale.

Money, shops & taxes

An artist and I owe each other money.

This happens when an artist kept the money from sales they made themselves, and you also owe them earnings. Choose their book and open the Dashboard: under Profit Sharing Breakdown, the Settle up panel shows both sides and the one payment that clears them.

  1. Send the artist the statement with Share statement so they see how it adds up.
  2. Once the money has actually moved, record it there. Saved it by mistake? Use Undo settlement.

If the artist pays with the card button on their own Dashboard, it’s recorded for you.

How do I pay back an artist’s expense?

Artists add costs in Expenses. The Dashboard shows Reimbursements due while any are waiting. Review each one (the receipt photo helps) before paying it back.

How do I keep track of shops holding books?

Use Consignment: add the shop, record the copies you dropped off, then record sales and payments as the shop reports them. It shows what each shop still holds and owes.

Where do I check money came in?

Payments lines up what customers paid against the sales in the books, so you can spot anything missing or doubled.

Where are my numbers for taxes?

Open Tax Centre for totals by year and expense category, ready to hand to your accountant.

Keeping things safe

Does an artist see my other books or the business totals?

No. An artist only sees their own book. Business tabs like Payments, Tax Centre and Customers stay with you.

How do I make a backup?

Open Backups to save a copy of everything. Do this before big clean-ups, like deleting old sales or changing many prices at once.

Two devices show different numbers.

One of them may still be sending changes made offline. Get both online, wait a minute and refresh. If they still disagree, open Backups — when the same thing was changed on two devices, the other version is kept there so you can compare and bring it back.

Trouble?

I have no internet. Can I still use it?

Yes. Keep adding sales as usual. They're kept safely on your device and sent automatically once you're back online. Just don't clear your browser data before then.

Can I put it on my phone like an app?
  • iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app.
A number looks wrong.

Wait a few seconds and refresh the page — it may still be syncing. If it's still wrong, tell the publisher which number and where you saw it.

A number looks wrong.
  1. Wait a few seconds and refresh — a change from another device may still be on its way.
  2. Choose the book and look through its sales in History. A sale typed twice, or with the price of all copies in the one-copy box, is the usual cause. Fix that sale rather than adding another.
  3. Two devices disagree? See Keeping things safe above.

What does the little “?” next to a box mean?

Tap or hover over it for a short explanation of what to type in that box.

Still stuck? Message the publisher — there's no wrong question.

Something here out of date, or an artist asked a question that isn’t covered? Ask for it to be added, so the next person finds the answer here.

    This v29 script syncs inventory events, scans Big Cartel order emails, and captures customer-paid shipping as separate Sheets rows for later shipping/postage analysis.
    Optional: send "Email artist for payment" from the app, not your Gmail
    By default this email sends from the Google account that owns the script (your Gmail). To make it come from a neutral address instead, sign up for a transactional email provider — Resend, Brevo, SendGrid, Mailgun, or Postmark — and verify a sender address. Then in the Apps Script editor open Project Settings → Script properties and add:
    • MAIL_PROVIDER — one of resend, brevo, sendgrid, mailgun, postmark
    • MAIL_API_KEY — your provider's API key (stays on Google's servers, never in this app)
    • MAIL_FROM — the verified sender, e.g. noreply@yourdomain
    • MAIL_FROM_NAME — display name (optional, e.g. Your Press Inventory)
    • MAIL_REPLY_TO — where replies should go (optional; leave blank to keep your address hidden)
    Mailgun also needs MAIL_DOMAIN (and MAIL_REGION=eu for the EU region). Redeploy after saving. If these aren't set, the email still sends from your Gmail as before.
