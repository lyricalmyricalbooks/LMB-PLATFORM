First questions
Can I bring my books over from my old shop?

Yes. Upload your old shop's file or a spreadsheet and your books, photos and customers come with you. Links to your old shop keep working.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You sign in with Google once and we set up your web address, payments and email for you.

What happens after the free trial?

Add a card to keep going. If you don't, your shop pauses and nothing is deleted. You can download everything at any time.

Can I sell at fairs too?

Yes. The stockroom works on a phone or tablet at the table, even offline, and takes the copies off the same count as your website.
