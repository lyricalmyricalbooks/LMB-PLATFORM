# Donations & tips, part three: tax receipts (G3, Beta)

From the approved plan "Four more ways to sell" (https://claude.ai/artifact/AJtw9ihHaKAg4B7EtBva9N), Giving G3: tax
receipts for registered charities, and a yearly summary in January. Split in two pull requests.

## G3a: receipts end to end
- [x] `src/shared/charityReceipts.ts` (server copy generated): a Canadian business number (123456789 RR 0001) or a US tax
      ID (12-3456789), the owner's details (`cleanCharity`; a Canadian charity needs a signer), the giver's address (whole
      or nothing, two-letter country), numbers per year (`2026-00012`, test gifts `TEST-…` on their own counter), and who
      gets one (`receiptEligibleMinor`: asked for, receipts on, no thank-you reward, a Canadian one with an address; the
      amount is the whole charge, a covered card fee included).
- [x] `functions/giving.js`: `givingReceipts/{year}` counters raised inside the paid transaction (a number is never given
      twice); the receipt in the thank-you email (EN/FR, the charity's wording for its kind); a refund cancels it; owner
      op `reissueReceipt` cancels and issues a new number naming the old one; `saveSettings` refuses a bad charity by field;
      `givingInfo` says which kind; `giftView` adds the charity when the gift has a receipt; `giftStart` takes `receipt`
      and `receiptAddress` (never with a reward; a Canadian one without an address is 400 `receiptAddress`).
- [x] Privacy: an erase keeps the issued receipt (a charity must keep copies) and drops the giver's copy of the address.
- [x] Admin › Donations: Amounts & choices › Tax receipts (switch, number with its kind, legal name, address, signer,
      January summary switch); a gift's receipt line (number, name and address, cancelled struck through) with Correct,
      which opens the correction drawer. Fixture: a charity, one issued and one cancelled receipt.
- [x] Support page: "Send me a tax receipt" with the address for a Canadian charity, a note instead when a reward is
      picked, and the receipt after giving with Print or save as PDF (printing shows only the receipt). Regions
      `supportReceipt`, `supportReceiptCard`; words Text & labels › Support page › Tax receipts (English and French).
      Studio shows it with `receipts=sample`.
- [x] Tests: shared rules (both copies), server (4 cases: settings, numbering and test numbers, rewards and US, refund and
      reissue), privacy.
- [x] Verified: screenshots of Donations (gift rows, settings with an error, correction drawer) and the Support page
      (receipt ask with a missing address, the receipt, print view) on desktop and phone.
