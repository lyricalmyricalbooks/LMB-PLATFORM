# Donations & tips, part one (G1, Beta)

From the approved plan "Four more ways to sell" (https://claude.ai/artifact/AJtw9ihHaKAg4B7EtBva9N), Giving G1:
a Support page with suggested amounts, a tip line at checkout, the option to cover the card fee, giving in someone's
honour, a choice to stay anonymous; gifts carry no sales tax and show in Bookkeeping apart from sales; protection
against card testing ($5 lowest gift, limits per visitor and per email, a bot check); the control room sees blocked
attempts; the switch shows as built.

Built in three pull requests, like Events:

## G1a: server, admin page, emails
- [ ] `src/shared/giving.ts`: the owner's settings (`cleanGivingSettings`: suggested amounts, other amount, cover the
      fee, honour, thank-you words), the gift's amount check ($5 lowest, $10,000 highest), the card fee estimate
      (`coverFeeMinor`), the giver's details (`cleanGiver`: name, email, anonymous, in honour or memory of, a note).
- [ ] `functions/giving.js`: public `givingInfo`, `giftStart`, `giftView` (through validateDiscountCode); owner ops
      through `givingAdmin` (`hint`, `list`, `saveSettings`, `resendThanks`, `refund`); `sweep()` in bookingSweep
      confirms paid card pages and closes unpaid ones. Records server-only: `gifts/{id}`, `givingSettings/main`,
      `givingBlocked/{day}`, `givingSecret/key`.
- [ ] Card-testing guard: $5 lowest, 5 card pages an hour per visitor, 3 an hour per email, a form token that must be at
      least 3 seconds old and a hidden field bots fill in. Every refusal is counted per day (`givingBlocked`).
- [ ] No sales tax: the card page charges the gift plus the covered fee, nothing else.
- [ ] Thank-you email to the giver (English or French), a short note to the owner.
- [ ] Admin › Gifts (Beta): totals, settings, the list of gifts with refund, blocked tries this month.
- [ ] Rules emulator: the new records stay closed to browsers, with a two-shop check. Privacy export and delete cover gifts.
- [ ] Tests: server (switches, $5 lowest, limits, bot check, paid once, refund), shared rules, admin view.
