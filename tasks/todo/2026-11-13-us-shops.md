# A shop fit for an American customer (13 November 2026)

Julian, 13 November: "Yes, add a US dollar test shop", "do #2,3,4 aswell" (US sales tax, shipping prices, stockroom
items with no currency) and "in general make sure the shop is completely fit for an american customer".

Follows `2026-11-13-shop-currency.md` (the shop counts in its own currency). This plan covers what else assumes Canada.

## What the sweep found (apps/shop)
- The shop knows its currency but not its country.
- Sales tax: Settings › Taxes opens with "Are you registered for GST/HST?", its presets are Canadian provinces, and the
  names in the books are GST/HST/PST. The rate table itself already takes US states.
- Shipping: label rates put Canada Post first and say "Canada Post" in errors and defaults; the starter shipping zone
  is Canada; weights are entered in grams and kilograms only.
- Payments: the manual payment choice is e-Transfer (Interac), which a US press cannot take.
- Words: "province" and "postal code" in the admin, the starting settings say Eastern Time and Canadian dollars.
- Time: the shop's day (sale dates, release dates, discount days, daily emails) is always Toronto's.
- Stockroom: an item saved with no currency reads as Canadian dollars (`apps/stockroom/src/lib/money.js`).
- Live carrier quotes are taken as the shop's currency without a check.

## The rule
A shop's country follows its currency for now: US dollars means the United States, anything else Canada (Julian,
12 November: a US customer has everything in USD, a Canadian one everything in CAD). One function,
`shopCountryOf(currency)` in `src/shared/shopCurrency.ts`, so a later per-shop country is one change.
