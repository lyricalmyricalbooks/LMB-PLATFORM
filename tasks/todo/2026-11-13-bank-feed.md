## Review
- Billing is an invoice line in arrears, like the running cost, so no Stripe subscription was changed. It matches how
  Plaid charges (per connected bank per month): `months[YYYY-MM]` keeps the most banks a month saw.
- Not built, on purpose: Plaid webhooks (the shop polls every 3 hours and on Check now), a line on the upcoming-bill
  preview, the pricing page's mention. A shop paying as it sells has no monthly bill, so its add-on line is skipped for now (as its running cost is): worth a decision before real shops use it.
