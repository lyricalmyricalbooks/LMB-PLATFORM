The platform backend

Your control room and the platform's server program: sign-in, billing, emails, the shop maker, the jobs that run by themselves, and releases. Plan 1 is the front door. Plan 3 is each press's own shop.

In short. The backend already does a lot, but all of it runs on pretend Google and Stripe, and the control room is strong at fixing one shop and weak at showing how the business is doing.

Today I fixed three real bugs and made the shop list faster. The new plan (Phase 19) adds the things big shop platforms give the people who run them: income over time, why presses leave, recovering failed payments, one timeline per shop, a jobs page, and asking a press before changing their shop.

Other companies' error messages no longer reach presses

Before: when Google, the email sender or QuickBooks refused something, the press saw that company's own English, and a Google refusal could read as "your sign-in ran out". Unexpected errors were never written down anywhere.

Now: the press sees plain words and a short code, like a3f09c1d. The same code is in the server's log, so you can find exactly what happened.

A cancelled plan can't reopen itself

Before: Stripe sometimes delivers its messages out of order. A late "plan updated" arriving after "plan cancelled" would put the cancelled plan back.

Now: a message older than the last one we took is ignored.

Cards can't switch themselves back on

Before: the same problem for a press's own payment account. If Stripe paused their cards, a late, older message could mark the cards as working again.

Now: the same rule, older messages are ignored.

The shop list and Today open faster

Before: the control room read each shop one after another, three reads per shop. At 100 shops that is about nine seconds of waiting.

Now: eight shops at a time, so the same page takes about a second.

The new plan: Phase 19

Rows 19.4 to 19.16 in the code plan, in the order I'd build them. None of it needs your accounts except where it says so. None of it touches your own shop or stockroom.

Safe and sturdy first

Each payment message handled exactly once19.4quick

If Stripe sends the same message twice at the same moment, both copies can run today. This makes the first one claim it, so a press is never emailed or charged twice.

Fair-use limits on every page call19.5medium

Only the sign-in link has a limit now. Without limits, one broken script or one bad actor can run up the platform's bill or slow it for every press.

The press says yes before "Make changes"19.6medium

Like Shopify's collaborator requests: looking stays as it is, but changing their shop sends the owner a request to approve. It builds trust, and protects you if a press later says "someone changed my shop". Closing a shop for abuse still works without it.

Know how the business is doing

Income over time19.7medium

A snapshot each night, and a 12-month line on Today: money coming in, presses gained and lost, and how many trials turn into paying presses. Without it you can't tell if a month was good.

Why presses leave19.8quick

One optional question when a press closes: price, a missing feature, the press closed, or something else. Answers grouped by month tell you what to build next.

Win back failed payments19.9medium

Stripe's smart retries and card updater (your switch), an email 30 days before a card expires, a one-click card page, and a list of shops paused for payment with days left. Failed cards are the most common reason a paying press quietly drops away.

See every shop clearly

One timeline per shop19.10medium

Billing, releases, health, support, switches, visits and emails in one list, newest first. When a press writes in, you see their whole story on one screen.

The jobs page19.11medium

Every job that runs by itself (health checks, backups, trial emails, tax watch) with when it last ran and a Run now button. A job that silently stops is the hardest problem to notice; this alerts you.

Payments and email delivery per shop19.12medium

Payment messages received and failed, emails sent and bounced, over the last week. A shop whose order emails bounce loses customers without knowing.

Notes and tags on a shop19.13quick

"Founding press", "needs a call", a short note. So you and your backup person remember the context.

Look after presses at scale

Messages to presses19.14medium

A banner in every shop admin (or one plan, or one shop) with an end date, plus an email for important ones. For new features, price notices and planned maintenance.

What each shop costs us19.15medium

Google's bill for each shop next to what that shop pays. A shop that costs more than it pays shows up before it hurts.

A press's data, on request19.16larger

One button builds a press's whole export (books, orders, customers, stockroom, settings) and emails a link for 7 days. Needed for leaving, for privacy requests, and for the 90 days a closed press has to download.
