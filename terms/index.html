Our promises

What we promise your brand, what we do with your data, and what happens to your shop if Colophon ever closes. All on one page, in plain words.

01 · Terms

The terms

You own your shop, your products and your customers. We keep it running and tell you before anything changes.
02 · Privacy

Privacy policy

We keep only what the shop needs, never sell it, and tell you and the people affected if something goes wrong.
03 · Data

Data agreement

Your data stays yours. You can download all of it, as spreadsheet files, whenever you ask.

If Colophon ever closes

You get plenty of notice, a full download of everything, and help moving your shop somewhere else.
