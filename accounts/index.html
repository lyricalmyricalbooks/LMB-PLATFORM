Delete my account

What happens

  • Your sign-in is deleted, and you can't sign in to Colophon with this address any more.
  • You leave the team of every shop you're on.
  • Sign-ups for shops that were never made are deleted.

What we keep

  • Invoices and billing records, for six years, because tax law asks for it.
  • Your past support messages and account recovery requests, as a record of what was asked.
  • Each shop's activity history, which shows who changed what in that shop.

For your safety this works only within 15 minutes of signing in. If it says so, sign out, sign in again and come back.
