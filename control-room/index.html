    Is Google ready?

    Prices and sign-up

    Changes here reach sign-up and new plans at once, with no release. A new price is for new subscriptions only; brands already paying keep theirs.

    Emergency payments

    Only if the platform's Stripe account is paused: move every live shop's card payments at once. Each brand is emailed straight away. Move them back the same way. One shop at a time is in its record.

    Breach register

    Write down every privacy breach, even one that didn't need reporting. Entries are kept at least two years and can't be deleted here.

    Write a new entry

    Shop ids, separated by commas; empty if none.

    Activity log

    Everything anyone did in the control room, newest first. Kept for a year; nothing here can be changed.

    Jobs

    Everything the platform does on a schedule: how often, when it last ran, how long it took and how it went. A job that stops running on time is emailed to the alert address once.

    Messages to brands

    A note at the top of every shop admin it reaches, until its end date. Shoppers never see it. An important one is also emailed once to each brand's owner. For outages, use the status page note in the run book.

    Promised notices sent

    Your proof of the 30 days' notice the terms promise: what changed, when each brand was told and how many owners were emailed.

    Costs

    This month's Google bill for each shop next to what the shop pays, the shops costing more than they bring in first. Credits are taken off.

    Run book

    For when the platform owner can't be reached: pause releases, roll back one shop, or post a note on the status page. The backup person opens this with a reason first; the owner is emailed at once, and every use after it.
