Presses at risk

Paying presses whose orders have dropped or whose team stopped signing in, checked every morning, with what they pay each month. Snooze one you've already spoken to: it leaves Today and gets no check-in email until the snooze ends.

Fair till by month

Sales rung up on the stockroom's fair till across every press, the last 12 months, newest first. One checkout of several books counts as one sale.

Failed plan payments

Shops whose plan payment is failing or paused for a card. A paused shop closes 60 days after the pause began; the press can then download everything for 90 days.

Support

Questions from presses. Your answer is emailed to them; close a request when it's done.

App store

Outside apps waiting for your review, and the ones presses can install.

Repairs

Problems the self-checks and the shop maker found that need a person. A check that passes again closes its own.

Account recovery

People who lost the email address their account uses. Approve only once you've checked it's them; the move then waits three days for the old address to object, and you make it by hand (accounts/README.md, "Recovery: applying").

Switches

Turn one feature on or off for every live shop at once, and choose what a new shop starts with. Each shop's own switches are in its record.

Is Google ready?

Prices and sign-up

Changes here reach sign-up and new plans at once, with no release. A new price is for new subscriptions only; presses already paying keep theirs.

Emergency payments

Only if the platform's Stripe account is paused: move every live shop's card payments at once. Each press is emailed straight away. Move them back the same way. One shop at a time is in its record.

Breach register

Write down every privacy breach, even one that didn't need reporting. Entries are kept at least two years and can't be deleted here.

Write a new entry

Shop ids, separated by commas; empty if none.

Activity log

Everything anyone did in the control room, newest first. Kept for a year; nothing here can be changed.

Jobs

Everything the platform does on a schedule: how often, when it last ran, how long it took and how it went. A job that stops running on time is emailed to the alert address once.

Messages to presses

A note at the top of every shop admin it reaches, until its end date. Shoppers never see it. An important one is also emailed once to each press's owner. For outages, use the status page note in the run book.

Costs

This month's Google bill for each shop next to what the shop pays, the shops costing more than they bring in first. Credits are taken off.

Run book

For when the platform owner can't be reached: pause releases, roll back one shop, or post a note on the status page. The backup person opens this with a reason first; the owner is emailed at once, and every use after it.
